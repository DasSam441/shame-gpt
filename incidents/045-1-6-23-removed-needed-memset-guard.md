# 045 — CarreraMod 1.6.23 deferred hooks but also removed the required original memset guard

**What happened:** 1.6.23 continued to crash. Although it deferred four TimTime functional hooks until the Cars screen, that build also removed the direct protection of the original ARMv7 memset PLT entry. The known null jump therefore remained unprotected.\n\n**Technical explanation:** The project evidence places the original null jump at libil2cpp + 0x04D81F08. Deferring TimTime hooks addressed premature installation, but removing the separate guard at that PLT entry restored the known failing path.\n\n**Why this was my mistake:** I bundled two independent changes—hook deferral and removal of the PLT guard—and failed to preserve the already evidenced protection while testing the new timing behavior.\n\n**Evidence and limits:** The 1.6.23 section of docs/CARRERAMOD_1.6.23_ARMV7_DEFERRED_FUNCTIONAL_HOOKS.md records the continuing crash and disassembly evidence; the 1.6.24 note describes the subsequent combined approach. The documents do not establish that the combined 1.6.24 build worked on a device.
